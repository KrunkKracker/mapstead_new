# Safety & Liability Notice

**JuMaSt Appworks LLC**

## Important Warnings
Mapping property infrastructure involves identifying potentially dangerous utilities, including gas lines, high-voltage electrical conduits, and pressurized water mains.

1.  **GPS Accuracy**: Coordinates provided by mobile phones (Phone GPS) are typically accurate to 3–15 meters. This is **NOT** precise enough for excavation, legal boundary determination, or critical engineering work.
2.  **Visual Reference Only**: Data in Mapstead should be treated as a visual reference. Always perform a physical "Call Before You Dig" (e.g., 811 in the USA) before any ground-disturbing activities.
3.  **No Guarantee of Completeness**: Mapped items represent only what has been manually entered. Unmapped hazards may exist.
4.  **Liability**: JuMaSt Appworks LLC is not responsible for any damage, injury, or loss resulting from the use or misuse of data stored in this application.

## Backup and Restore
`Phase 2A — Paused: implementation preserved, customer access disabled pending destructive restore testing`
The Google Drive Backup & Restore feature is currently **disabled** in customer release builds. Current customer builds do not request Google Drive authorization or upload backup data.

When enabled for development/testing:
- A full restore will **replace** your current local data.
- Mapstead creates a local safety backup immediately before a restore operation.
- Always verify the integrity of restored coordinates and instructions before relying on them during an emergency.

## Best Practices
- Use high-accuracy sources (e.g., professional survey data) whenever possible.
- Clearly label accuracy sources for every point (e.g., "User Estimated" vs. "Imported Survey").
- Physically verify all shutoff locations during non-emergency periods.
